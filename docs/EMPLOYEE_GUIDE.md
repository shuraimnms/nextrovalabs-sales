# Employee Guide

## Signing in
Use the email and temporary password your manager gave you. At first sign-in you must choose your own password
(≥ 10 characters, with upper case, lower case and a number). Forgot it? Use **Forgot password**, or ask an admin for a reset link.
Five wrong attempts lock your account for 15 minutes.

## Find anything fast
Press **Ctrl/Cmd + K** anywhere to search scripts, objections, WhatsApp templates, knowledge articles and courses.

## Live Call (your main tool during a call)
1. Choose the **service** the customer is asking about and the **stage** of your call (opening, discovery, budget, objection, closing…).
2. Pick or search **what the customer says**. You get a recommended Hinglish line, an alternative, and **things to avoid**.
3. Keyboard: **←/→** next/previous script · **A** show alternative · **F** favourite · **Esc** leave focus mode.
4. Use **Focus mode** and the text-size control when you need a cleaner, larger view.

Anything marked **Pending confirmation** has not been approved by an admin yet — do not quote it as a company commitment. Never promise prices, delivery dates or guarantees unless an admin has confirmed them.

## Scripts & Objections
Browse by service, stage and difficulty; star your favourites; **Copy** puts the text on your clipboard. Each objection has four versions
(recommended, short, detailed, alternative) plus why it works and what not to say.

## Academy
12 courses, each with lessons and a quiz. Mark lessons complete as you go. Your **certificate** is issued only when you have completed
**every lesson and passed the quiz**; it has a verification code (NSOS-…) you can show a manager. You can retake quizzes.

## Roleplay
Practise against a customer persona (e.g. busy, price-sensitive, sceptical) for a project type and difficulty. Up to six of your replies per session, then you get scores on six criteria with feedback.
The screen says whether replies come from the **rule-based** simulator or an **AI** provider. Only your scores are saved — not what you typed.

## WhatsApp templates & Knowledge
Templates come in English and Hinglish with a copy button — personalise before sending. Knowledge articles cover company, services, process and sales basics.

## Settings
Theme (light/dark/system) and default text size. Change your password under Settings → Account (other devices are signed out).

## What this system is not
It does not store customer details or contact customers for you. Keep customer information in the tools your company already uses for that.
